## 2. Executive Strategy: The WSO2 Blueprint for Early Growth

1. **Code Freeze on `zeoraz.com`**:
   * The platform is already production-ready (<0.4s load, 99/100 Core Web Vitals, Gemini AI chat, validated contact form). 
   * Additional CSS/code tweaks do not generate revenue. Focus 100% on client acquisition.
2. **The Single Beachhead Offer**:
   * Instead of confusing clients with a menu of 10 services, pitch one outcome:  
     **"The Next.js Sub-Second Speed & 24/7 WhatsApp AI Lead Engine" — \$1,500 Flat.**
3. **The "Zero Phone Calls / 100% Async" Operational Model**:
   * Founders do not need to take awkward phone/Zoom calls.
   * Conduct 100% of business via **WhatsApp voice notes, text, and 60-second screen recordings (Loom)**.
   * Frame this as an elite feature: *"We work 100% async to save you time and build your platform in 7 days."*
4. **The Sales Co-Founder / Closer Partnership**:
   * Recruit an energetic business/marketing student from **AIESEC / SLIIT**.
   * Offer a **25% cash commission** on every closed client (\$375 on a \$1,500 deal).
   * They handle all prospect conversations; Shabaab handles 100% of the engineering.
5. **B2B White-Label Agency Subcontracting**:
   * Partner with digital marketing and SEO agencies in Colombo, Dubai, and the UK.
   * You build the websites and apps behind the scenes; the marketing agency manages the client relationship.
